About Ashok Courier Services

A legacy of precision, trust, and speed. Connecting 19,500+ pin codes across India and 220+ international destinations.

Our Journey & Evolution

Pioneering Reliable Express Logistics Since 2008

Founded with a singular commitment to redefine courier and package transit, ASHOK COURIER SERVICES began operations with just three delivery vans and a passion for punctuality in New Delhi.

Over the past eighteen years, we have scaled relentlessly. Today, our multimodal logistics infrastructure encompasses over 1,200 dedicated delivery vehicles, high-speed regional linehaul trucks, dedicated air freight agreements, and 180+ automated sorting hubs.

We handle everything from high-priority diplomatic documents and temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical samples to bulk commercial freight and e-commerce parcels, serving over 2.8 million satisfied clients.
