Volumetric Rule

How Is Volumetric Weight Calculated?

In air freight and express courier transport, packages with high volume and low weight occupy significant cargo bay space. Courier rates are billed on the higher of Actual Weight or Volumetric (Dimensional) Weight.

Standard IATA Industry Formula:
Volumetric Weight (kg) = (Length × Width × Height in cm) / 5000

For example: A lightweight pillow box measuring 50 cm × 40 cm × 30 cm = 60,000 / 5000 = 12.0 kg Volumetric Weight, even if the actual scale weight is only 1.5 kg.
